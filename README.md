# DumDev

**A local X automation bot.** DumDev uses a local Ollama model and a browser you choose to find relevant conversations, create posts, and publish contextual replies within the limits you set.

DumDev is the X bot. **Cruise Control is a separate HeyClicky companion**—they are not the same product.

## At a glance

- Choose your browser and X account during setup; settings and activity stay on your Mac.
- Arc supports X search, posts, and contextual replies with visible foreground control. Brave and Chrome support standalone posts only.
- The local dashboard at `http://127.0.0.1:8765/` shows activity, status, errors, and recovery information.
- After setup, configured activity can publish automatically; per-draft review is optional. Daily caps are limits, not quotas.
